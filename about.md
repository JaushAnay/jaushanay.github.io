Bad data rarely looks bad. It looks like a finished report with a confident number at the bottom. I am Joshua Anayochukwu, a Data Validation Specialist at Sportmonks; a global sports data provider, and my job is to catch those problems before anyone builds on them. I check match lineups, squads, leagues and season records so customers get football data they can rely on.

Checking before trusting has become a habit, and it runs through all of my work. I analyse data with Excel, SQL and Power BI, and design the pipelines and PostgreSQL databases that keep data reliable as it moves. I build AI automation workflows with n8n and Supabase that take repetitive checking and reporting off people’s plates. My portfolio shows the range: a validation system for football data, a hospital database I cleaned and analysed, a retail data pipeline, a payments reconciliation system, and automations that catch errors early. I write about this work on Medium, because explaining a problem clearly is part of solving it.

Work with me and you get someone who questions the numbers before trusting them, explains them in plain language, and builds systems that stop the same mistake from happening twice: automated checks, pipelines that flag bad records early, and reports nobody needs to double-check. Explore my projects and articles below. If you need data you can trust, let’s talk.
