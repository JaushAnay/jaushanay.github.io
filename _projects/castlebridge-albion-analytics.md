## Why I'm building it

Clubs make decisions across departments that rarely share one view of the numbers. This project brings sporting, financial, operational and commercial data for one club into a single analysis.

## The club

Castlebridge Albion FC is fictional: a Champions League club I created so the data could cover every part of a club's operations. It also appears as an English top-flight side in [Football DQ Lab](/projects/football-dq-lab/).

## What it will show

- Data cleaned and modelled in Excel.
- Analysis across the four areas of the club.
- Power BI dashboards that answer questions a club director might ask.
