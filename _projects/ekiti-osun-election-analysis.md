## Why I'm building it

Election results are published in fragments, formats change between sources, and small transcription errors can change a story. This project shows the full analytics process on civic data: collecting it, cleaning it, validating it, analysing it, visualising it and explaining it.

## Scope

The 2026 off-cycle governorship elections in Ekiti and Osun states. The project is strictly analytical and non-partisan: it reports what the data shows and how it was checked, not who should have won.

## Process

1. **Collect** published results from public sources.
2. **Clean** them into one consistent structure.
3. **Validate** totals and cross-check figures between sources.
4. **Analyse** results and patterns.
5. **Visualise** them in Power BI.
6. **Communicate** findings in a plain-language write-up.
